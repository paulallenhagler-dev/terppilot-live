# TerpPilot discovery and editorial rules

The Explore homepage provides three user-intent entry points: Vibe (exploratory terpene patterns, not effect predictions), Flavor (aroma-associated terpene tags, not sensory guarantees), and Science (specified primary/secondary terpenes and minimum concentration). The app displays mathematical profile similarity, not a likelihood of outcomes.

**TerpPilot's Take** currently uses transparent rules grounded in the individual batch's reported terpenes and cannabinoids. It is not an external language-model call. Write-ups must not invent taste notes, claim that a product was personally sampled, or promise sleep, focus, creativity, anxiety reduction, or other effects. Unreported compounds are unknown, not zero. Human-readable flavor notes should remain specific and restrained: citrus, floral, pine resin, earthy/herbal, peppery/woody spice.

The catalog is historical and batch-specific. Retail inventory has not been integrated, so do not label source-product pages as current in-stock purchase links. Any future generative-AI pipeline must be server-side, reviewable, reproducible, and tied to the exact COA and model/version. Never place API secrets in browser-side JavaScript.
